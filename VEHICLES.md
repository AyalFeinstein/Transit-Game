# Vehicle Details

This document describes all available vehicles in GoCommuter, organized by category.

## Vehicle Attributes

Each vehicle has the following key attributes:
- **Name**: Vehicle identification
- **Year Introduced**: When the vehicle becomes available
- **Fuel Type**: Coal, Wood, Oil, Electricity, or Food
- **Top Speed**: Maximum speed in mph
- **Average Speed**: Typical operating speed in mph
- **Miles per Unit**: Fuel efficiency
- **Maintenance Costs**: Annual maintenance cost
- **Base Price**: Base purchase price (before inflation)
- **Quality**: Vehicle quality rating (affects line quality)
- **Capacity**: Passenger capacity (for self-contained units)
- **Max Cars**: Maximum railcars that can be attached
- **Can Have Railcars**: Whether railcars can be attached
- **Needs Overhead Wire**: Requires overhead wire infrastructure
- **Needs Third Rail**: Requires third rail infrastructure

## Vehicle Selection Guide

### Early Game (1830-1850)
- Start with horse-drawn vehicles or early steam locomotives
- Use Wooden Coach Cars for capacity
- Focus on basic infrastructure

### Mid Game (1850-1920)
- Upgrade to improved steam locomotives
- Use better passenger cars (Crackerbox, BU, Harriman)
- Consider electric streetcars in cities

### Modern Era (1920-1980)
- Transition to diesel or electric locomotives
- Use modern passenger cars
- Consider subway systems for dense areas
- EMUs for regional service

### Contemporary Era (1980+)
- High-speed locomotives and EMUs
- Modern passenger cars with high quality
- Advanced subway systems
- High-speed ferries for water crossings

## Important Notes

- **Self-contained units** (EMUs, DMUs, streetcars, subway cars, ferries) cannot have railcars attached
- **Electric vehicles** require infrastructure: overhead wire or third rail
- **Quality** affects line quality, which increases ridership
- **Capacity** determines how many passengers a vehicle can carry
- **Fuel efficiency** (miles per unit) affects operating costs
- **Maintenance costs** are annual expenses that must be paid
